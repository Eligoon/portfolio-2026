## Week 1 - setting up shop.

**What did I add this week?**

I set up the basic structure for the projekt in paper format. I began writing down entities and forming up the idea around the project of what I wanted it to be.

I asked my clients about what they wanted the application to have of functionalities as well as what they might have of "nice to have" functions besides the MVP (minimal viable product), depending on how much time I would have left at the end of the project. 

I began making and working on a domain model over the application as well as deciding on a name for the application: Oniscidea.


**Which new technology did I use?**

I used good old fashioned paper to put all my ideas of the project, as well as my clients needs and ideas down into one collection. From there I could work on the ideas and form a picture of where the project should head.


**Why did I structure it this way?**

I prefer knowing what I go into before I start anything. As many details and set in stone things as I could make, the better. 

While I need to leave some things open to be able to scope and work on it, I do prefer to get certain things down before even starting to touch any code.

**What worked well?**

My clients had a lot of ideas and wishes for funtionality of the application. To this working in a structured and with open communication has put me in a good starting position. 

I am always tryin to think three steps ahead, so I have already instrected my clients to think ahead of what they might want from the front end as well as specific other details I might need from them in the future.

**What was difficult or confusing?**

Diving into new terms, code syntax and having no idea how it really works together yet is always demanding. 

**What would I improve next time?**

I would wish I had prepared better for school start in the summer vacation. Summer vacation and getting back from it is always. 

I am having problems attending enough days, so I have to compensate by doing a lot of research on my own and having been a bit unsure on some of the technical things.
